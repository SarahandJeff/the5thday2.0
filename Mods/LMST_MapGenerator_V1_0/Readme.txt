MapGenerator_Fix - 1.0.0 - Gameversion: V2.5
This mod fixes the map generator bug that has been occurring since full version 2.5, where hardly any dead ends, curves, or straight roads are generated in cities. Please keep in mind that this is a DLL injection and that the anti-cheat must be disabled. After you have generated a new map and are satisfied with it, you can remove the mod again and continue playing with the anti-cheat enabled.
